# Metric definitions

## Active customer
A customer with a live subscription at the start of the period.

## Churned customer
A customer whose subscription was cancelled during the period.

## Churn rate (monthly)
Churned customers in the month ÷ active customers at the start of the month.
Example: 50 ÷ 1,000 = 5%.

## Retention rate
1 − churn rate. The share of customers who stayed.

## MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
Sum of monthly subscription charges of all active customers.

## Churned MRR
MRR lost from customers who cancelled in the period.

## ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)
MRR ÷ number of active customers.

## Cohort
A group of customers who signed up in the same month. We track each
cohort over time to see how many are still active.
