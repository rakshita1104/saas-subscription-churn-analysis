# SaaS Subscription Health & Revenue Intelligence

## Business problem
Customers are leaving a subscription product. Why is it happening,
how much revenue is being lost, who is at risk next, and what should
the company do to fix it?

## Questions this project answers
1. What is the monthly churn rate and how does it change over time?
2. How much recurring revenue (MRR) is lost to churn?
3. Which customer segments churn the most?
4. Does low usage predict churn?
5. If churn falls by X points, how much revenue does the company keep?

## Tech stack
Python (pandas, scikit-learn), DuckDB, SQL, Power BI / Tableau
